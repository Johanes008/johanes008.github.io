Fasting Time privacy policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Fasting Time ("the app") is an intermittent fasting timer made by Johanes ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • Your fasting data (fasts, times, notes, feelings, protocols) stays on your phone. We have no server, and we never receive it.
  • If you turn on Google Drive backup, a copy goes to a private folder in your own Google Drive. Only the app, signed in as you, can open it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage information to show and measure ads, as described below. Your fasting data is never shared with Google AdMob or any ad network.

Information on your device

Everything you log in the app is stored in a database on your phone. It works fully offline and without an account. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

If you turn on notifications, the app schedules its reminders (fast target reached, eating window closing and so on) on your phone with Android's own notification system. They are created on the device from your fasts; nothing about them is sent to us or to anyone else.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google" in Settings:

  • The app uses Google Sign-In to learn your Google account's email address, which it keeps on your phone to show which account you are backed up to.
  • It asks for access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive only (the drive.appdata permission). It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • It saves one file, fasting_time.json, with your fasts (including deleted ones, marked as deleted, so other phones remove them too), your custom protocols, and your default and last-used protocol, and keeps it in step with your phone. Your theme and other device settings are not included.

This data goes directly between your phone and your Google account; it does not pass through any server of ours. Google's handling of your Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Fasting Time's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can stop backup at any time with "Disconnect Google account" in Settings (your data stays on your phone). To delete the Drive copy, remove the app's access in your Google Account under Security, then "Third-party apps & services", and delete its hidden data from Google Drive's settings under "Manage apps".

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear on the History screen and, occasionally, after you finish a fast.

To show, limit and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID;
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • information about how you interact with ads, and app diagnostics such as crash and performance data.

Google uses this under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. The app never passes your fasting data, notes, or health information to the ads SDK, and ads are not targeted using it.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states that require it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, under "Ad privacy choices".
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings, then Privacy, then Ads, on most Android phones).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
